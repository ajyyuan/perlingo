About this deck

Common words. Clear meanings.

1,500 Spanish study cards selected using the RAE / ASALE CORPES frequency table, with explicit teaching edits. This is a practical corpus-based selection, not an exact top-1,500 ranking or a promise of 90% coverage.

What counts as a card

Each card teaches a headword and grammatical use. Poder the verb (“can”) and poder the noun (“power”) have separate cards. There are 1,455 distinct spellings across 1,500 cards. Selected verb forms are pronunciation aids and do not add to the count. A word can have other senses not shown here.

Selection and edits

We preserve the order of eligible source entries, excluding punctuation, capitalized names, numeric strings, phrases, contractions and letter names. We include intereses with interés and fill its slot with the next eligible entry, religioso.

Seven teaching headwords replace ambiguous source labels: música, característica, salida, lista, perspectiva, deuda and caída. Their cards explain the change under “About this headword.” These are editorial choices; we cannot recover the exact frequency of each intended meaning from the aggregate table. Downloaded counts still belong to the original source entry. Card numbers are study order.

The table was retrieved October 25, 2026; it does not identify its extraction version. Corpus annotation and dictionary matching are imperfect. Meanings use a frozen Wiktionary-derived dictionary with concise editorial corrections; selected verb forms were checked against its form mappings. This is scoped editorial review, not independent linguistic certification or contextual mastery testing.
