About this deck

Common words. Clear meanings.

1,500 Spanish study cards selected using the RAE / ASALE CORPES frequency table, with explicit teaching edits. This is a practical corpus-based selection, not an exact top-1,500 ranking or a promise of 90% coverage.

What counts as a card

Each card teaches a headword and grammatical use. Poder the verb (“can”) and poder the noun (“power”) have separate cards. There are 1,455 distinct spellings across 1,500 cards. Inflected forms help recognition and do not add to the card count. A word can have other senses not shown here.

Selection and edits

We preserve the order of eligible source entries, excluding punctuation, capitalized names, numeric strings, phrases, contractions and letter names. We include intereses with interés and fill its slot with the next eligible entry, religioso.

Seven teaching headwords replace ambiguous source labels: música, característica, salida, lista, perspectiva, deuda and caída. Their cards explain the change under “More about this word.” These are editorial choices; we cannot recover the exact frequency of each intended meaning from the aggregate table. Downloaded counts still belong to the original source entry. Card numbers are study order.

The table was retrieved October 25, 2026; it does not identify its extraction version. Corpus annotation and dictionary matching are imperfect. Meanings use a frozen Wiktionary-derived dictionary with concise editorial corrections; selected verb forms were checked against its form mappings. This is scoped editorial review, not independent linguistic certification or proof of comprehensive contextual mastery.

Word notes

All 1,500 entries have lexical notes and grammatical help. All 1,500 now have authored sentence examples and a contextual review question. The reviews use 537 distinct sentences, sometimes with different words blanked. Each question tests one use and form; it does not test every sense or inflection. Nine tense/mood reference groups are supplied for all 369 verbs, with third-person-only tables for three event/state uses and explicit restrictions on commands. Conjugation tables are adapted from English Wiktionary contributors via Kaikki/Wiktextract (October 1, 2026 dump), under CC BY-SA 4.0; changes include person/tense selection, compact labels, compound-tense assembly and targeted RAE-based corrections. These reference tables do not add conjugation tests. Gender and agreement forms come from dictionary metadata, explicit exceptions and checked inflection mappings. An initial gloss pass corrected 104 learner meanings. A second screening changed ten entries after targeted RAE comparisons, including plural and agreement errors missed by form-membership checks. Most entries have not had a separate second-dictionary verification. These checks do not establish exhaustive sense coverage or error-free content.

Your practice

Thirty sets of fifty, arranged across five suggested days. Learn ten words, type their Spanish headwords, then complete a multiple-choice set test. Each day has a full 300-word typed test and a 300-word sentence review. Finish each pass, retry every miss, then complete a fully correct full pass; the first-pass score stays separate. Days 2–5 link to the previous day’s sentence review. You can pause and resume. Guest progress saves in this browser; signed-in course progress syncs with your account; old 50-question pilot results remain archived separately. Pronunciation uses your device’s available voices. Optional microphone dictation sends audio to Azure Speech through the existing speech service.
