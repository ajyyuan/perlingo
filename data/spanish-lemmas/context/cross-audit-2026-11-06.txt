Confirmed correction needed
1278 escapar: “Para escapar, ve directamente hacia la puerta verde.”
- escaparse currently earns credit.
- escaparte currently gives a spelling retry.
- huir correctly earns credit.
The person addressed by ve is tú. The pronominal alternative must therefore be escaparte. Both reviewers independently found this before discussing findings. RAE's grammar glossary requires pronominal agreement in person/number: https://www.rae.es/gtg/verbo-pronominal . For the subject of purpose infinitives, see https://www.fundeu.es/consulta/sujetos-en-oraciones-finales-de-infinitivo-289/ . This is a sentence-form correction, not grounds to remove dictionary-form escaparse from typed recall.
The other pronominal additions morirse, reírse and quedarme fit their respective subjects.
