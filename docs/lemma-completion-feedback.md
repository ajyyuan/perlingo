# Completion feedback

A newly completed batch, set, day test or context review receives a brief checkmark entrance and quiet synthesized three-note resolution. It plays after Continue clears the final question, never merely from restoring or revisiting a completed stage. Reduced-motion preferences disable entrance animation. The existing Sound preference controls answer feedback and completion chimes; pronunciation settings are unchanged. Lemmas correct/wrong-answer and completion cues have a 20 dB gain increase (10× amplitude) over their initial levels; pronunciation volume and legacy-app cue levels remain unchanged.

Take a break returns to Your days as before. There is no timer, break dialog or scheduled chime.

## Local review

Open `/lemmas/completion-preview.html` for an isolated preview using the production component without altering course progress. Click Replay completion to hear/see the cue. Check reduced-motion mode. In the course, check Sound off suppresses completion audio and Take a break returns to Your days.
