Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026. Perlingo is maintained by Andrew Yuan. Contact reports@perlingo.app with privacy questions or requests concerning your account data.

Learning without an account

Guest progress, preferences and unfinished lessons are stored in your browser. Perlingo does not upload guest learning progress to its account database. Your browser still connects to the hosting service to load the app, which can process technical connection information such as your IP address and request details.

Google and Apple sign-in

If you sign in, Google or Apple supplies identity information to our authentication provider, Supabase. This includes a provider account identifier, email address and any basic profile information supplied by the provider, such as a name or profile image. Apple may supply a private relay email address. Perlingo uses this information to identify your account, show your sign-in status and associate saved progress and reports with you. The app does not request access to your Google mail, contacts, calendar or Drive files.

Authentication sessions are stored in your browser to keep you signed in. Your Google or Apple password is entered with that provider, not into Perlingo. Signing out on one browser does not sign you out on other devices.

Saved progress

For accounts, Supabase stores a private progress snapshot containing preferences, learning and review history, session records and unfinished-day state. A copy is also saved in your browser. Sync uses these records to resume lessons and resolve competing changes. Before replacing progress through a device/cloud choice, the app keeps a recovery copy in that browser.

Content reports and support

When you submit a content report, we store your account identifier, report time, your note, the lesson version, relevant teaching content and your submitted answer when applicable. The maintainer can read reports to investigate and correct content. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Report notification emails contain a report identifier, not your note, answer or identity. Resend delivers these notices, and Cloudflare forwards them to the maintainer’s Gmail inbox. If you email us directly, your email address and message are processed by the mail services and read by the maintainer to respond.

Service providers and use

Perlingo uses Sites hosting to serve the app, Supabase for authentication and account storage, Google or Apple for optional sign-in, and the email services described above. These providers process information needed to deliver their services and may process technical or security logs under their own policies. Account data is hosted in the United States in the configured Supabase region.

The current app contains no advertising or third-party analytics integration. Google identity information is used for the account features described here, not for advertising. Pronunciation uses your browser or operating system’s speech service; depending on the selected voice, that service may process the text remotely. The app does not record your microphone.

Retention and your choices

There is currently no automatic expiry for saved accounts, progress or reports. Signing out does not delete cloud data or the browser’s saved account draft. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of account data; there is no self-service account-deletion screen. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Provider logs and backups have their own retention schedules.

You can export current progress from Progress and the latest recovery copy from Manage account. Clearing Perlingo site data in your browser removes its local copies and session; it does not remove cloud copies. Downloaded exports remain wherever you save them. You can also revoke sign-in access through your Google or Apple account settings; revocation does not itself delete data already saved with Perlingo.

Updates

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
