About this review

Use Left/Right to move through the filtered language tiles (wrapping within the selected word). Space starts/stops speaking; L plays the recording. Enter cycles Unchecked → Checked → Needs attention → Unchecked. Shortcuts are inactive in notes, dropdowns and links. Unchecked is gray, Checked is green, and Needs attention is amber; labels remain visible. Uses the selected cached recording, the server’s game speech route, and the game’s answer matcher. Speak naturally; silence finalizes speech as in the game. Stop flushes the current attempt; capture stops after 15 seconds. Microphone audio goes to the provider shown before you start. No audio is saved. Transcripts, notes, and manual checks stay in this browser and appear in exports. A checked tile is your review, not an automated pronunciation certification. Recording, wording, or provider changes require a new review. This page does not change scores or daily attempts.
