Record three seconds once, then send the identical audio to both providers. Try the correct word, a wrong word, silence, and a self-correction. Recordings stay in memory until you leave; comparison sends them to ElevenLabs and Soniox.

Latency is measured after the recording finishes streaming, including finalization. Connection time is separate. This test finalizes once at clip end; game endpointing can behave differently. A few samples cannot establish an accuracy rate.
